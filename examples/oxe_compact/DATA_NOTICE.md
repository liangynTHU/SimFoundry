# Data notice

The files under the `droid_episode_*` directories are compact derivatives of
DROID episodes distributed through Open X-Embodiment. They are data samples,
not NVIDIA-authored source code, and the repository's Apache-2.0 software
license does not by itself relicense the underlying observations.

The samples are included for research, reproducibility, interface testing, and
simulation/reconstruction experiments. Users and redistributors are responsible
for reviewing and following the upstream DROID/Open X-Embodiment dataset terms,
privacy guidance, citation requests, and any restrictions that apply to their
use case.
